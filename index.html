Why retailers partner with Skarrel

01
Reach shoppers already comparing. Everyone browsing Skarrel is actively deciding where to buy right now — a special posted here reaches people mid-decision, not mid-scroll.
02
No design team, no media buying. Post a special in under a minute — store, price, branch, expiry date — and it's live on the board and on every matching product page.
03
A trust-verified board. Only signed-in retailers can post, so shoppers aren't wading through anonymous noise to find your real promotions.
