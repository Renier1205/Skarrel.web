how it works

Three steps, every time you shop

No account tricks, no fine print — just the same three steps whether you're grabbing milk or doing the full week's shop.

01

Search or scan

Look up a product, or snap a photo of last week's receipt — Skarrel reads the retailer, branch, and every price automatically.

02

See who's cheapest

Every price is shown store by store, brand by brand, filtered to branches actually near you — not just a national average.

03

Track what you saved

Add picks to your trolley and Skarrel tallies your savings by month, so the pattern of shopping smarter actually adds up.

kept honest, together

Built by shoppers, checked by shoppers

Skarrel doesn't guess at prices — every one comes from a real person, at a real till, and stays accurate because the same community can flag what's wrong.

🧾

Prices from real receipts

Shoppers add prices by hand or scan a till slip — Skarrel reads the retailer, branch, date, and every item for you to check before it's added.

📍

Every branch, not just the brand

Prices and specials are matched to your actual nearest branch, and shoppers can flag a wrong or outdated one so the map stays accurate over time.

👍

Confirmed or disputed, out loud

Anyone can confirm a price is still right or flag one that isn't — enough reports and it's marked disputed before you rely on it.

🔖

Real specials, from verified retailers

Only retailer accounts Skarrel has personally verified can pin a special to the board — so shoppers aren't wading through anonymous noise.

for retailers

Reach shoppers who are already deciding where to buy

Skarrel sends shoppers to your branches and your site — it's built to work alongside retailers, not around them.
